What Illinois loses to legal gambling, from the state’s own records.

The Illinois Gaming Board publishes, to the dollar, what is played and lost at every licensed video gaming location, casino and sportsbook in the state. These pages turn those monthly reports, and the lottery’s audited figures, into one running reference: losses, taxes, growth, and what the state spends on treatment. Compiled and checked by Chris Beerman, LCSW, CADC, a gambling addiction therapist in Chicago.

How this is made

Public records only

Every figure comes from a document anyone can check: Illinois Gaming Board monthly reports, the Commission on Government Forecasting and Accountability’s annual Wagering in Illinois update, the Auditor General’s lottery audits and Department of Human Services releases. Each page ends with its sources and methods, and says where Illinois publishes no data at all.

No industry money

This site takes no funding, advertising, data or affiliate income from gambling operators, suppliers, affiliates or bodies they fund, and carries no tracking beyond the hosting provider’s logs. Pages carry the date they were last reviewed, and corrections are made in the open. The standards in full.

Kept by a clinician

Chris Beerman, LCSW, CADC, treats gambling disorder, sports betting and high-risk trading at Chicago Addiction Therapy in Logan Square and edits the Gambling Research Index. He keeps these pages because the numbers clients bring in deserve the same care as the clinical work. About the editor.
